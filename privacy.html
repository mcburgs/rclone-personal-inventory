Privacy Policy

Rclone Personal Inventory is a personal, non-commercial configuration of the open-source rclone desktop utility, used only by the owner of the connected Google account.

Data accessed

When the account owner grants Google OAuth consent, locally running rclone may read Google Drive file names, paths, identifiers, sizes, timestamps, checksums and, where the authorized scope allows, file contents. An independently authorized backup configuration may upload and manage files within its granted permissions.

Purpose and use

Access is used solely for the account owner's file inventory, comparison, verification and personal backups. Data is not used for advertising, profiling or commercial purposes.

Storage and transfers

Rclone operates on the owner's computer. Its configuration, OAuth credentials, inventory reports and operational logs are handled locally according to the owner's chosen settings. File transfers, when initiated by the owner, occur between the owner's computer and Google Drive. This informational website does not receive, collect or store Google Drive files, metadata or credentials.

Sharing

The operator of this personal integration does not sell Google user data or disclose it to advertisers or third-party data brokers. This site does not include analytics, tracking scripts or user-submitted forms. Google processes data as part of providing Google Drive; rclone is open-source third-party software running locally.

Control and revocation

The account owner controls which folders and operations rclone accesses. The owner can stop using rclone, remove local configuration files, or revoke the application's Google access through Google Account permissions.

Changes

This notice may be updated if the local application's data practices change. Last updated: November 2026.
